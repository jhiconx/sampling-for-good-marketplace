Quick questions

Is this a paid internship?

We're recruiting for paid campus internship opportunities. Compensation, availability, eligibility, schedules, and any applicable employment terms will be discussed with selected applicants.

Do I need a large Instagram following?

Not necessarily. Strong campus relationships, reliability, communication skills, and organizational experience are valuable. Instagram helps us learn about your campus presence.

Will I personally ship products?

You'll help coordinate delivery information and follow up with brands and organizations. Actual shipping and fulfillment responsibilities depend on the campaign.

Do I need to log in with Instagram?

No. Add your handle to your application. Instagram account access and passwords are not required.
