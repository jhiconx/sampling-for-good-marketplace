Chapter-based ambassador compensation

Onboard chapters. Support events. Earn as they succeed.

Your chapter-based payments are earned at three specific milestones. Each chapter's first three events have their own payment window.

🤝
$25

One-time payment per signed-up chapter

Arrange an introduction and meeting between our team and a university chapter. When that chapter completes its qualifying platform signup, you receive one $25 payment. Scheduling a meeting alone does not trigger the payment.

📦
$1

Per unique sample redeemed — first 3 events

For each chapter you onboard, receive $1 for every unique sample redemption during that chapter's first three sampling events only. After its third event concludes, you no longer receive redemption payments for that chapter's future events.

🏁
$500

One-time campus completion bonus

Earn a final $500 bonus when every chapter on your pre-approved university roster completes registration and its first three sampling events. The qualifying chapter roster is agreed upon in writing before work begins.

The written compensation plan will define qualifying chapters, the approved campus roster, unique redemption validation, payment timing, and compensation legally required for time worked. Incentive payments do not replace any wages owed under applicable law.

Quick questions

Does setting up a meeting earn $25?

No. The $25 payment is earned once a chapter attributed to you successfully completes its qualifying registration and is approved. Introducing the chapter and arranging the meeting are part of the role.

How are sample redemptions counted?

You earn $1 for each verified, nonduplicate unique sample redemption tracked by the platform during a chapter's first three sampling events. The exact redemption eligibility and validation rules will be provided in the written compensation plan before work begins.

What happens after a chapter's third event?

Your $1-per-redemption payments for that chapter end. You can still earn signup and eligible first-three-event payments from other chapters you onboard.

How do I earn the $500 campus completion bonus?

You earn the one-time $500 payment after every qualifying chapter on your university's agreed, approved roster has completed registration and its first three sampling events. The roster must be confirmed in writing before work begins.

Can I work around my classes?

Chapter meetings, onboarding, training, and event support are planned around classes where feasible. Any work time that must legally be paid will be compensated according to applicable wage-and-hour requirements.
