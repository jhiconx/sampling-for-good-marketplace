Try. Verify. Give. Retarget.

Five steps from chapter table to checkout.

1

Recruit & stage

Confirm chapter hosts and ship brand-approved sample kits before the event.

2

Try together

Members experience Final Boss Sour or Rogue during a chapter event.

3

Verify the handoff

Students scan a QR code and complete a unique claim via mobile.

4

Give back

Each eligible verified claim funds the approved philanthropic component.

5

Retarget to purchase

Students who opt in receive a relevant DTC link or a participating-grocer offer.

SMS marketing requires separate valid consent and applicable disclosures; receiving a sample does not automatically constitute marketing consent. Donation beneficiaries, timing and terms require approval.

More than a free sample

The sample is the beginning, not the finish line.

A verified event gives brands an attributable sampling touchpoint. Permission-based follow-up can then direct students to the purchase destination most relevant to the campaign.

Track sample claims, opt-ins, messages delivered, link clicks and offer redemptions. Report online transactions or in-store redemption only when the applicable brand, retailer and attribution integrations are in place.

Path A · Buy online →

Brand-approved offer by SMS → tracked brand website or checkout link → attributable order where technically integrated.

Path B · Buy at a grocery store →

Store-specific offer or locator → participating physical retailer → POS-verified redemption where integrated.

Path C · Keep the conversation going →

Optional product education, new flavor announcements and later campaigns for students with valid marketing consent.

Illustrative 70-chapter pilot economics

At 150 samples per chapter: 10,500 verified handoffs × $1.75 = $18,375.00.

At 200 samples per chapter: 14,000 verified handoffs × $1.75 = $24,500.00.

These calculations assume all distributed units are verified and apply to one brand's handoff count. The budget is not a combined two-brand quote. Actual billing is based on contract terms and confirmed eligible redemptions.

Brand-specific pitches

Different products. The same measurable engine.

Make the chapter the sour challenge.

Use a shared tasting moment to generate peer-to-peer discovery, then invite students to discover more levels or flavors through the brand's approved e-commerce experience.

  • Sampling creative: chapter sour-taste challenge and QR claim.
  • Optional follow-up: try another flavor or discover the full sour lineup.
  • Purchase destination: approved Final Boss Sour DTC offer or confirmed grocery availability.
  • Success measures: verified handoffs, opt-in rate, qualified clicks and attributable purchases where integrated.

Show that protein snacks can still bring the flavor.

Build discovery around Rogue's chip and puff flavors at campus chapter gatherings, with relevant follow-up that supports repeat snack purchases.

  • Sampling creative: flavor trial and QR claim at chapter gatherings.
  • Optional follow-up: favorite-flavor poll and approved online offer.
  • Purchase destination: Rogue DTC checkout or confirmed nearby retail locations.
  • Success measures: verified handoffs, opt-in rate, qualified clicks and attributable purchases where integrated.
